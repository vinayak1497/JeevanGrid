Phase 01 • BEFORE Preventive

Build a 72-Hour Family Kit

When standard supply chains and power grids snap, having vital supplies pre-packed prevents immediate desperation.

  • check_circle 4 liters potable water per person/day
  • check_circle Non-perishable high-protein food & dry dates
  • check_circle Prescription medicines & waterproof ID copies
  • check_circle Crank/solar radio & external power bank
Family Readiness Index Step 1 of 3
Phase 02 • DURING Active Danger

Execute Survival Postures

Knowing split-second reactions reduces injuries by up to 80%. Stay calm and adhere to official advisories.

  • check_circle Disconnect main gas valves & electric trip switches
  • check_circle Move away from unreinforced masonry & glass panes
  • check_circle Do NOT cross inundated bridges or fast culverts
  • check_circle Follow designated evacuation routes from collectors
Shelter & Egress Plan Step 2 of 3
Phase 03 • AFTER Recovery

Safe Recovery & Hygiene

Secondary hazards like contaminated water and hanging live cables often present greater risks post-event.

  • check_circle Boil all tap water or use chlorine purification
  • check_circle Photograph structural damages for claim filings
  • check_circle Signal stranded elders or pets with bright fabric
  • check_circle Rely strictly on official bulletins; reject rumors
